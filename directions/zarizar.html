Overview

ZariZar

ZariZar, a luxury Indian shawl company, partnered with Parsons School of Design to evolve its market strategy, brand identity, and web design. I developed the brand identity alongside two teammates and took ownership of the web design.

Our direction centered on the legacy of Deccan craftsmanship and the women who keep it alive, bringing the stories behind the shawls into the brand and digital experience.
